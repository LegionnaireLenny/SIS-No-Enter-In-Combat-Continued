[h2]What is this?[/h2]
This mod disables the ability to enter buildings while in combat. Can be applied to only rbe player, the whole community, or everyone.
By the default cofiguration, only the controlled player (and any local players) can't enter buildings while under attack. They can however enter buildings that belong to their community.

Can be added to current saves.

[h2]Settings[/h2]
The settings file can be found in the mod folder under "Settings_NoEnterInCombat.xml"
[list]
[*] EnabledForPlayer. Whether the mod is enabled the player. (default true)
[*] EnabledForCommunity. Whether the mod is enabled for your community members. (default false)
[*] EnabledForNPCs. Whether the mod is enabled for NPC's. (default false)
[*] CanEnterCommunityBuildingsInCombat. Whether the characters can enter buildings in combat that belong to their community. (default true)
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Shouldn't have any incompatibilities. Report any issues in the comments section!
